# Assets and attribution

The demo room, floorboards, furniture, plant, sound-source model, listener marker, reflection overlays, brand mark, material swatches, and test floor-plan fixture are original procedural geometry/SVG/CSS created for Echo Cartographer. No downloaded photographs, room meshes, or proprietary textures are used.

Both sound samples (pitched percussion and filtered noise), the calibration sweep, and the illustrative stereo reverberation are synthesized locally by this application. They contain no sampled recordings or licensed music.
